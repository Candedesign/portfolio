ONBOARDING

Onboarding Process: From Complexity to Simplicity

One of the main pain points in the onboarding process was its complexity and the time it took users to complete it. To address this, the onboarding experience was redesigned to simplify the process and integrate the new Design System.

The redesign included user registration and login, company and bank account forms, collaborator management, a summary screen, and post-submission editing.

As part of the process, Interbanking’s Visual Designers worked together to analyze the strengths and weaknesses of the existing components. Based on this analysis, we identified the need to create new components and patterns, which were later incorporated into the Design System for use across all teams.

PLAN USAGE

New Plan Usage Interface

With the introduction of the new AI assistant, the monthly plan usage components were redesigned to provide a clearer view of the services included in the company’s plan, while also incorporating AI usage tracking through a token counter.
