THE PROJECT

Hebamio

Hebamio is a digital solution designed to support midwives (Hebammen) in managing their work across Germany and Austria. It allows midwives to document, manage, and bill for their services, including pregnancy, childbirth, postpartum, and newborn care, streamlining the administrative and clinical tasks they perform every day.

ROLE

UX/UI Designer

TIMELINE

2024

SKILLS

Visual Design
Component Creation
UX Design
Prototyping

A new desktop version was designed, introducing new features and improving the overall user experience. The redesign makes it easier for midwives to monitor the health of both the mother and the newborn, manage their services, and keep track of relevant information throughout pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum care.

Designed and Coded by María Candela Marchese
