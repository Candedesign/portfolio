THE PROJECT

ProVida MetLife

ProVida AFP is one of Chile’s leading pension fund administrators, with over 40 years of experience and more than 3.3 million members nationwide. Today, as part of MetLife, ProVida combines strong local expertise with the backing of a global leader in insurance and retirement solutions.

DIGITAL EXPERIENCE

A new homepage experience designed for Pensioners

The homepage was redesigned for pensioners, allowing users to check their pension amount and next payment date, change their payment method and location, download certificates, and complete tasks online without having to visit their local ProVida branch.

PENSION REQUEST

A complex process made easy

The pension application form was fully redesigned to allow users to complete the process online, independently, quickly, and easily. Users can select the savings they want to include in their pension, choose a health plan, add beneficiaries, and set up their preferred payment method.

PROVIDA FUNDS

Fund Change and Distribution interface

The interface was fully redesigned to make the process more visual and intuitive. I worked on the visual representation of the funds, using distinctive imagery and colors to make them easier to recognize and understand.

WITHDRAWAL PROCESS

A faster and transparent withdrawal process

A process that used to be complex for the majority of users, now with the redesigned flow, users can withdraw money from their APV or Cuenta 2 accounts, either in cash or through a bank account deposit, in a faster and simpler way.

SECURITY

Safer accounts with 2 factor authentication

Two-factor authentication was added to the platform, allowing users to securely complete transactions such as withdrawing money, opening an account, or updating their profile information using a verification code sent via SMS or email.

DESIGN SYSTEM

A Design System with ProVida identity and MetLife foundations

With the creation of the new site design, building a Design System was essential not only to unify the experience across the site, but also to ensure that every component was aligned with MetLife’s brand identity.

A foundation file was created with ProVida’s own design tokens, using parameters equivalent to those of the MetLife US Design System. This established the foundation for building ProVida’s new Design System. The Design System was developed for over a year by a multidisciplinary team of designers and front-end developers, who also built and documented the components in Storybook.

PROVIDA APP

A New Experience for the ProVida App

The ProVida app was completely redesigned, introducing new features and improving the overall user experience through a refreshed look and feel, a new color palette, illustrations, and micro-animations.

User Testing

Compare Funds and make better decisions

A new feature that allows users to compare the performance and returns of each fund, understand where their money is invested, and choose the option that matches their preferences

Turning Savings into Personal Goals

A new simulator feature that allows users to set how much they want to save each month or year to reach a personal goal, such as buying a house or a car, or planning a trip.

ProVida End-of-Year Summary

A personalized summary highlighting the user’s key milestones, achievements, and recommendations to help their savings grow and encouraging users to keep saving to reach their goals. Built around the concept of an “adventure,” the experience changes based on each user’s fund and investment profile: higher-risk investments lead to a more adventurous journey, while conservative funds offer a calmer experience.
