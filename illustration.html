About the project Design Studio Workshop Medium Pre-sales

THE PROJECT

Globant Illustration Domain

A specialized illustration area within Globant’s Design Studio. Its main role is to manage illustrators and illustration requests from different areas across the company, such as Presales and Medium articles. The team is also responsible for organizing activities and workshops related to illustration.

As part of the staff, I collaborated with a dedicated design team on the Summer Camp 2025 social media campaign, brand identity development, and internal communications, with the main goal of attracting young students.

DESIGN STUDIO

Creating the studio identity

In October 2023, the Design Studio organized a brainstorming session with all the designers to develop the concept for the studio’s first mascot. I was in charge of creating the final artwork, which was later used across the company’s internal communications, including posters, postcards, and presentations.

WORKSHOP

Character Design Worksop

In March 2024, I led the Character Design Workshop as part of the Illustration Domain’s monthly workshops. The main goal of this activity was to expand the reach of illustration beyond the design teams. The workshop was open to any employee across the company interested in illustration, encouraging creativity and diversity.

MEDIUM

Illustrations for Medium Articles

As a member of the Illustration Domain, I was in charge of creating illustrations for Medium articles written by featured Globant designers.

PRE-SALES

Presales Storyboards

I led the Storyboard initiative for the Illustration Domain, coordinating a team of illustrators in the creation of characters, backgrounds, and key visual elements (props). Providing visual solutions for a wide range of industries, including transportation, EdTech, AI, retail, and pharma.
